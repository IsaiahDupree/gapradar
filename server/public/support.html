Getting started

  • Create your account at gapradar.vercel.app — everything runs in the cloud, so there's nothing to install.
  • Add GapRadar to your assistant. In ChatGPT, Claude, or Codex, add a custom MCP connector with the server URL https://gapradar.vercel.app/mcp and set authentication to OAuth.
  • Authorize by signing in with your GapRadar account and clicking Allow, then ask it to find gaps.

Common issues

  • Connector shows unauthorized. Remove and re-add the GapRadar connector in your assistant to refresh the OAuth grant, then sign in and click Allow again.
  • "You've hit your plan limit." The Free plan includes 5 opportunity checks per month. Upgrade to Builder, Pro, or Studio from the plans page for more scans.
  • A tool says it needs a paid plan. Gap detection, review clustering, and build-dossier generation are available on Builder and above. Search and comparison work on the Free plan.
  • Store data looks incomplete or out of date. GapRadar reads only public app-store data through licensed/approved sources; some regions or stores refresh on a delay, and not every app is listed in every store.
  • An app appears on one store but not another. That's expected — a workflow that exists on one platform and not another is exactly the kind of gap GapRadar is built to surface.

Cancel / revoke access

  • Remove the GapRadar connector in your assistant's settings to stop it from calling GapRadar.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from the Stripe customer portal via the plans page.
  • Email us to delete your account and all associated data.
